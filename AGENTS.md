# Prototype Instructions

## Durable product and design decisions

- The local customer-service demo uses a dark-green navigation sidebar, quick questions and authorized orders, a central conversation, and a live diagnostic column. Keep its controlled single-agent architecture. Show actual model/tool/validation outcomes; public research JSON stays pending review and excluded from retrieval until approved. See `docs/customer-agent-local-demo.md` for the local-only boundaries.

- 登录前预加载画面按端固定：网页端继续使用原“晴天”逐帧页面，小程序登录前使用用户提供的“雨天”五帧动态页面；不要依据实时天气在这两个入口切换画面，也不要将画面名称显示成实时天气预报。

- Keep a visible test-account picker on the localhost desktop login page and a “切换测试账号” action after login. Shortcuts only fill seeded credentials and use normal server authentication; retain existing passwords, account status, orders and drafts. Gate the helper on a development build, loopback browser host and a backend confirmed local nonproduction database/origin. Production builds and mobile readonly views must omit the helper and its credential bundle.

- The product name is `苔的跟单旅程`. It and its source code are personally owned by the developer; company business data remains company-owned and may only be processed after the signed authorization in `docs/launch/data-authorization-template.md`.
- For cloud testing, production initialization, release/restore work or pilot acceptance, read `docs/launch/README.md` and follow its task-specific runbooks. The active test route uses a Tencent Cloud Shanghai Lighthouse server, self-hosted PostgreSQL 17, private COS buckets and a new company-filed domain. The cloud account's holder must match the actual account registration, filing conditions and signed authorization; TCR Personal Edition does not establish account ownership. Cloud testing uses fabricated data in an isolated database that will never become the formal database. The developer retains ownership of the product and code; `norli.com.cn` and the existing company website must never be changed. Sites remains a static prototype artifact. Public exposure and employee access require their recorded filing and acceptance gates.
- The approved pilot is <=50 internal employees and one group with 10–20 new orders; old orders and their supplements stay in the old process. Both processes share the numbered pools via audited old-process reservations; never bulk-seed sample orders into production.
- Production cost access defaults off until separate cost acceptance (`COST_MODULE_ENABLED=false` at both API and UI). Enable cost only in the isolated company cloud test environment for full-workflow testing. Keep local cost samples and reviewed data intact; desktop acceptance still gates all Mini Program implementation.

- This is a desktop-first internal order follow-up system. The first public release adds `/mobile` only as a chairman-administrator read-only web view; its dedicated server-side session rejects all business writes. WeChat Mini Program work remains phase 2 and paused until desktop/mobile web acceptance.
- The cloud route targets RPO <= 6 hours and RTO <= 8 hours. PostgreSQL dumps run every six hours to a separate private, encrypted, versioned COS backup bucket. The application credential cannot access backups; attachment and backup credentials cannot delete objects. Verify these targets by isolated restoration before claiming them.
- The later Mini Program phase is intentionally limited to designer `设计下单` work, workshop QR production reporting, installation records, exception photos, aftersales handling, and role-scoped order quick views. It remains paused until desktop/mobile web acceptance and its own truthful service category and filing are accepted; otherwise retain the mobile web route.
- Formal-order production numbers use `YYMMCSSS`, where `C` is the category digit and `SSS` is a monthly sequence shared by the same digit: 诺尔丽正单=1、诺尔丽补单=0、艾可居正单=9、艾可居补单=8、渠道正补单=5、工程/工装正补单=7. Every supplement prefixes its independently allocated base number with `补单-B`, producing `补单-BYYMMCSSS`, and retains an immutable `parent_order_id` link.
- A sales order receives one immutable sales number on formal design submission, formatted as brand prefix + `YYMM` + four-digit monthly sequence: 诺尔丽=N、艾可居=A、渠道=C、工程=E、工装=W. Supplements receive their own sales number from the same brand-family pool. Design rejection and resubmission preserve it; `TEST-SALES-*` samples never consume formal pools.
- A sales order may have multiple production areas. Technical staff create each area after pre-review and the server immediately allocates that area's existing-rule production number; area names and numbers lock on whole-order technical submission. Cancelled area numbers remain audited and are never reused. Order-level production number is compatibility-only.
- Production-area cards on order detail are a single-selection navigation surface. Selecting one area shows only that area's workshop processes below; never render all areas' long process ledgers at once.
- Generic demo orders use explicit `TEST-DEMO-*` identifiers and must not seed, increment or reset formal number pools. Preserve already assigned order numbers and their history. The local 2026-08 Norli sequence jumped to 98 due to the earlier demo seed, then allocated 099–101; this is test-data contamination, not the intended starting sequence. Real-Excel acceptance samples keep their user-supplied source numbers for matching.
- The customer due date is manually entered by sales from the customer agreement and is never generated from the site type. Sales chooses exactly one site category: 精装修、毛坯别墅、毛坯平层. The internal maximum production window starts only when the designer formally submits and generates the sales number: melamine orders use 33/37 calendar days for finished/rough sites, while paint orders use 38/42; all production areas share this order-level deadline. Both rough categories share the rough base, and rough villas require at least one floor with the existing +10 days per floor rule. Sunday is the only non-working day for internal tasks; internal deadlines landing on Sunday move to Monday.
- Sales row scope has two levels: a sales representative sees only their own orders within their assigned brand, while a sales supervisor sees every formal/supplement order of their brand, including legacy orders created by an administrator or assigned incorrectly. The order business type is the brand authority; never infer its brand only from the salesperson relation. Sales cannot create cross-brand orders. Each brand's sales team uses the same canonical business-unit code; account forms must select canonical values. Local scope testing uses two additional representatives per brand and preserves existing credentials, account status, orders and drafts. Both rules are server-side; department-wide visibility does not let a supervisor submit payment reports in place of the order's salesperson.
- Rush orders require sales to record a reason and promised date. Show the rescheduling impact before the order administrator applies it; rush never bypasses reviews or shipment gates.
- The earlier inventory-reservation and standard-stock replenishment design is historical only and must not appear in the active Procurement V2 UI or create new records.
- Active desktop surfaces must not show inventory, shortage, replenishment, stock-reservation, supplier/RFQ, purchase-order, receipt, or delivery-readiness reminders. Procurement V2 shows only the all-order procurement register, the panel and wood-paint route gates sharing one 15-working-day deadline, their server-timestamped completion facts, and separate cost status.
- Procurement V2 deliberately has no online inventory, replenishment, supplier, RFQ, purchase-order, receipt, or return workflow. These activities happen offline. For `ROUTE_V2`, procurement confirms each production area's panel route and wood-paint route independently; every confirmation is persisted immediately and releases only that area's selected route. The UI may offer a one-click action that confirms all remaining areas of one route kind through the same audited operation. The final applicable area-route confirmation automatically writes the historical whole-order procurement-completion fact. A first released route may enter production while other area routes remain pending, and costing never blocks release. `AREA_LEGACY` retains the former whole-order confirmation.
- The active system has no dedicated warehouse employee role or warehouse login. Workshop QR reporting is performed by the four workshop roles; offline receiving or storage activities do not create a warehouse task or an online warehouse user. Keep the legacy `WAREHOUSE` enum and historical data read-only for compatibility only.
- The real warehouse material sheet confirms that the hardware workbook contains two structured regions: the upper material/board/edge-band/glass area and the lower 诺尔丽五金配件 area. Parse both from the Excel display values, split multi-quantity hardware rows into child material lines, and never treat handwriting added to printed sheets as automatic-import data; handwritten corrections must first be entered into the source Excel and pass preview/review.
- Procurement and material costing are independent lines. Four dedicated workbook inputs—hardware materials, paint/wood, solid-wood door, and veneer door—use fixed template fingerprints and parsers. Technical approval publishes their combined immutable material version, creates a cost version, and stores the four reviewed workshop planned quantities. Unknown template structures must be rejected, never guessed.
- Material cost matching is exact on normalized name + specification + unit. New keys become warning-marked unpriced catalog items; procurement or the chairman records versioned final RMB unit costs. Unpriced orders show a priced subtotal but no official total. Orders snapshot prices; later price changes do not rewrite history unless an explicit audited recalculation is requested.
- Warranty starts at final customer acceptance: 诺尔丽 20 years and 艾可居 10 years, including supplements from their own acceptance date. 渠道、工程、工装 have no automatic warranty calculation in the first desktop release.
- The authenticated desktop business UI uses the earlier Black Lake-derived palette: a near-black deep-green sidebar, teal primary actions and selected states, a neutral light-gray page background, white cards and modals, dense tables, saved role/status views and compact toolbars. Keep scrapbook textures, dusty rose surfaces and cartoon illustrations out of post-login business pages. The login keeps its current journal layout and orange-cat interaction, but its canvas uses the same `#06251f` deep forest green as the authenticated sidebar with a restrained small-white-dot pattern; all decorative pink and rose accents are replaced by dark green or green-gray. Its cat pupils must remain clipped inside the eye sockets and follow the latest pointer once per animation frame without easing lag. Map left, right, up and down gaze independently against the available viewport distance from the eye center, so the off-center cat can still use the full safe eye range in every direction. Calibrate the hand-drawn eye boundaries asymmetrically: preserve the accepted near-edge travel to the right and down, keep the accepted left clearance, and keep at least 7 CSS pixels of clearance from the top eye line at the upward extreme. Clicking the cat, or activating it by keyboard, gives the whole cat one short gentle wiggle. Use the yellow yarn-ball cursor across the login surface only. `/mobile` follows the restrained business palette without decorative motion unless separately approved.
- The latest approved desktop login layout uses a white canvas with small dark-green dots, the brand centered in the left half, and the orange-cat login card on the right. Keep about 48 CSS pixels between the card's right edge and the outer frame on desktop, including short viewports. Preserve the stacked narrow-screen layout.
- Wide desktop tables must stay inside their cards: keep cell text on one line, define column widths, provide an obvious bottom horizontal scrollbar, and pin important action columns instead of allowing buttons to overflow the content boundary.
- On the desktop login form, place the displayed title `苔的跟单旅行` around the vertical center of the left side and the orange-cat login card on the right. At a 16:9 desktop viewport, keep the full cat, card and decorative frame inside the visible page. The localhost test-account picker remains available without covering the form.
- The order-detail `发货执行` card always stacks the manufactured whole-order shipment track above the outsourced-product shipment track, matching the accepted single-column layout at every desktop width; never switch these two tracks back to a side-by-side grid.
- Order detail grid columns and their nested cards must shrink to the available width; material-preview tables scroll within their card. At desktop widths of 1240px or less, stack the timeline/warranty after the main content instead of squeezing the technical workspace into two overlapping columns.
- The technical material-preview card starts directly with its upload slots, without a large explanatory banner. Keep the category filter, error checkbox and upload status vertically centered with explicit spacing and wrapping; retain validation and error feedback.
- The order-level outsourced-product declaration card starts directly with its declaration and quick-add controls; do not place a large explanatory info alert above ordinary office-form controls. Preserve validation and actionable error feedback.
- Do not show an internal draft-version badge on the order-level outsourced-product declaration card; saved state is communicated by the save action and restored values. Four-workshop production plans and reporting use area in square metres (`㎡`) for panel, wood, paint and assembly; outsourced finished products remain quantity-based in `件`.
- In the Procurement & Cost all-order register, keep the sales number alone on the first line of the fixed identifier column and show `tracking serial · customer name` together on its second line.
- Technical submission confirmation must keep its material table and alerts within the modal width. Scroll wide tables horizontally inside the preview and long form content vertically inside the modal; reserve visible right-side clearance so an overlay scrollbar never covers form fields, section frames or text. Keep the step header and submission actions visible within the viewport.
- The technical-submission form must not show redundant alert boxes explaining that production areas are filled separately or repeating the currently selected area. The production-area cards are the only area selector; do not render a duplicate dropdown. Keep clear vertical separation between the production-area card picker and the following area-data validation section. Every active area's required design-version, route and applicable workshop-plan fields must be complete before the form can proceed beyond step one, while the validation summary remains optional. Preserve the underlying per-area draft isolation.
- Technical submission history and review show Chinese business summaries and a read-only table of the submitted material snapshot, with quantities, units and source locations. Never expose raw material JSON, parser parameters or internal batch IDs in that view; retain the original payload and attachments for audit.
- Improve legibility over the reference: larger base text and targets, direct action labels, status text paired with color, and no ambiguous progress percentages.
- All business date-time fields use the unified Chinese date-time picker. Keep the `确认时间` action and its guidance inside the open calendar/time panel, compact the panel so its footer stays inside an 837px-high viewport, and commit the pending value only through that in-panel action; required-field validation remains active.
- Manufactured-shipment installation plans, installation completion, customer acceptance signatures and shipment records use dates only: show a date-only calendar, commit immediately when a day is clicked, and omit hour/minute selection and a separate confirmation action. Warranty starts from that acceptance date. Sales deposit/tail-payment receipt dates also use date-only selection and YYYY-MM-DD storage/display, including finance verification; preserve historical receipt and shipment records. Audit timestamps retain precise date-time values.
- Completing installation is an installation-department action. Order administrators must neither see the action nor submit it through the API; the chairman-administrator retains its explicit override authority. Preserve historical installation records.
- Workflow forms with no uploads or checklist content use two steps only: business information, then confirmation/submission. Keep the three-step information/evidence/confirmation flow only when the middle evidence step contains real attachments or checklist work.
- The customer-final-acceptance workflow keeps its signature upload step but uses a compact modal; its signature date and conclusion controls are equal-width, equal-height columns on the business-information step, and its single-file evidence step must not retain the generic tall empty-content minimum height.
- The order detail page is the center of the product. It must surface the current owner, next action, deadline, payment gate, material/purchase state, production handoffs, installation issues, attachments, and timeline.
- A tracking serial exists before the sales number. Until design submission, the tracking serial is the primary list/detail identifier. The designer's formal submission generates the brand-prefixed sales number, which then becomes the order's primary identifier; the original tracking serial stays searchable and visible as a secondary identifier. Production numbers identify production areas only.
- Sales assigns the designer before signing. The `设计下单` task becomes actionable only after both the assignment and finance-confirmed initial payments exist, and is due seven calendar days later. Before `ACCEPT_DESIGN`, the current designer may return a `SALES_STANDARD` order to its owning sales representative with a reason; internal-exception orders keep their assigned designer and never expose or accept `RETURN_DESIGN`. The owning sales representative, same-brand sales supervisor, or chairman may then assign an active designer to the returned standard order and start a fresh seven-day intake window. After `ACCEPT_DESIGN` and before formal design submission, the current designer or chairman may transfer the task to another active designer; transfer keeps the original `designAcceptedAt`, 1–7-working-day commitment, and `designDueAt`.
- HaiXun integration is structured Excel/CSV import first; API is optional later. Imported engineering data requires preview, validation, second-person review, and immutable version publication.
- 生产执行只包含板式、木工（实木）、油漆、组装四个车间。五金和外购不参与生产进度。外购成品由技术声明订单级清单、订单管理员按实际批次累计登记到货数量与日期，每个产品齐套且尾款确认后可独立发货；董事长管理员保留覆盖权限，采购与财务均不得代为登记。这不是库存或普通物料收货流程。外购到货登记弹窗使用等宽的两列表单行：左侧标签对齐，右侧数量与日期控件等宽；单位显示在数量输入框内部，使数量框与日期框边框同高。
- 销售从“新建销售签约订单”录入整套合同、客户约定交期并分配设计师；设计师在订单内提交一份或多份 CAD 设计资料（DWG、DXF、PDF、XLS、XLSX）、客户确认图和版本，并在正式提交时生成销售编号。复尺完成日期与客户确认日期保留为业务字段，空间清单摘要和材料/颜色/特殊工艺说明均选填；不要求上传复尺记录或空间柜体清单。客户样块允许多份图片、PDF或HEIC共用一条备注，有样块时备注必填，没有样块时选填。订单管理员随后进行前置审单，退回时保留已生成的销售编号。设计资料不强制预绑定生产区域。
- The first workflow stage is `签订合同`. When creating a new formal order, sales records two separate initial-payment facts with configured receipt account, amount, actual receipt date and evidence: the deposit may predate the contract, while the prepayment belongs to contract signing. Finance verifies both on one page and the order reaches design only when both are confirmed. Historical orders retain their earlier single-deposit policy without fabricated prepayments.
- Internal exceptional orders are a creation mode, not a business type or top-level workflow state. Active designers, technical splitters, order administrators and chairman-administrators may create one without an assigned salesperson; all sales users and supervisors are excluded from its row scope. The creator records only a charged/free proposal and reason. The order administrator atomically confirms either a positive contract amount plus one reported deposit for finance verification, or a reasoned free order that creates no payment facts and explicitly satisfies every payment gate. Internal charged tail payment is reported by the order administrator and verified by finance. Free orders retain the complete downstream lifecycle and four-part cost accumulation, while revenue and gross margin are not applicable. Payment amounts for internal charged orders are visible only to order administrators, finance and chairman-administrators; their receipt files are accessible only to order administrators and finance.
- Payment reporting and payment verification are separate facts. Sales records deposit, prepayment and tail-payment facts; finance may only review those reports and confirm money actually found in the corresponding account. Never treat an unverified sales report as received cash.
- Contract amount is visible to the assigned designer, finance, every sales user whose row scope includes the order (including same-brand supervisors), order administrators and chairman-administrators. Customer-payment amounts remain visible only to the assigned salesperson, finance and chairman-administrators; payment receipts remain downloadable only by the assigned salesperson and finance. Order administrators and chairman-administrators may change a formal or supplement contract amount only before any manufactured/outsourced shipment and before tail-payment reporting or verification; require optimistic concurrency/idempotency, enforce the applicable positive/nonnegative lower bound and confirmed-initial-payment floor, and append an audit event instead of rewriting history.
- Deposit, prepayment and tail-payment receipt files are uploaded only by the order's assigned salesperson. Only that salesperson and finance may list or download those files; chairman-administrators, order administrators, sales supervisors or other salespeople, designers, technical staff and all other roles receive no receipt-file access. Keep high-level payment amounts and verification status separate from receipt-file confidentiality.
- Finance order detail omits the completed four-workshop process ledger once finance is handling the order. Its basic information shows finance-confirmed deposit and prepayment separately; tail receivable is contract amount minus both confirmed initial payments. If either required initial payment is unconfirmed, no formal tail receivable is produced.
- Deposit plus prepayment may not exceed the contract amount. Preserve older inconsistent facts without rewriting them, but show a blocking financial exception instead of silently clamping the tail receivable to zero.
- Finance initial-payment and tail-payment verification are single-page direct submissions. Initial-payment verification shows both sales reports and requires separate actual-receipt confirmations for deposit and prepayment before one atomic submission advances to design.
- 台面专项由拆单/物料缺口自动创建，从“生产执行 → 外购”处理需求资料、供应商报价、财务审价、发单、到货、安装签字与对账。
- Production completion uses a QR-driven, low-literacy workflow: the worker selects the exact workshop process and reports start, completion, or an issue with large buttons. Every process is checked independently.
- The first shipment is blocked until finance confirms the full balance payment.
- Installation issues, production/design responsibility issues, and customer additions create independent child supplement orders. Each child links to the parent order, receives its own brand-family sales number on design submission and one production number per later-created production area, records charge/responsibility type, and runs the complete lifecycle without changing the parent order's immutable facts.
- Create supplements either from the formal order detail or by selecting a searchable, same-family formal order. The server is authoritative: it rejects supplement-to-supplement and cross-family links, inherits customer/address/site/sales/designer from the parent, and never accepts a freehand parent identifier.
- Workflow actions are evidence-backed business submissions, never blind confirmation buttons. Design submission requires versioned CAD and customer confirmation files, offers an optional customer-sample upload, and does not require survey or space-list files; pre-review and technical review require explicit item-by-item checklists and comments; technical splitting requires the selected production routes, their workshop plans, and at least one validated material workbook category. HaiXun sheets, decomposition sheets, and the validation summary are optional; when uploaded, the two sheet types are order-level shared references. Payment, production, installation, delivery and acceptance require their corresponding facts and attachments. Persist every submission, checklist, review decision and attachment reference in the order record before advancing workflow state.
- Every evidence-backed workflow form supports a server-persisted incomplete draft. Saving current progress may preserve any subset of fields, checklist items and uploaded attachments, and never advances the order stage; only the final validated submission advances workflow state. The single-page pre-review form must retain a visible “保存当前进度” action: save any subset of checks and the review comment without completeness validation, restore them on reopening, and require all checks only for formal approval.
- Workshop execution is cumulative quantity reporting, not an all-or-nothing completion checklist. Each released process independently records start, partial completed quantity, full completion or an issue and displays `completed quantity / planned quantity`. In `ROUTE_V2`, each area has independent panel→assembly and wood→paint→assembly routes; procurement, production and shipment may overlap, and each completed route may ship after the tail-payment gate while other routes continue. Percentages come from the shared process→route→area→order projection.
- When an actual completion exceeds its planned deadline, keep the completed state and display a separate `延期 N 天` marker; reminders stop only because the task is complete, while the delay remains reportable.
- When technical splitting finds an unmanufacturable design, technical staff must create a separate `Vx-Txx` revision. The original design remains read-only, the splitting task pauses, and the original designer must confirm the technical revision before splitting resumes.
- Procurement and finance/cost are first-class areas. Only finance and the chairman-administrator role may see gross-margin values.
- Do not create a separate top-level Finance & Cost page. Finance payment work is handled from personal/department tasks and order detail; material cost management stays inside the role-scoped Procurement & Cost workspace.
- Order cost accounting has five components: material-list cost, workshop cost, installation cost, outsourced finished-product cost, and cumulative valid aftersales cost. Material-list cost starts after technical approval publishes the formal material list. Workshop cost is snapshotted per production area at technical approval from the area's applicable planned workshop area multiplied by the effective versioned comprehensive rate: panel ¥12/㎡, assembly ¥3.4/㎡, wood ¥80/㎡, and paint ¥60/㎡. Installation cost is the sum of every manufactured shipment batch's installation fees and transport fee. Finance or the chairman records versioned, order-specific outsourced-product unit costs for the approved product list; each line uses its approved planned quantity, and any missing unit cost leaves the grand total provisional without blocking fulfillment. Material, workshop, installation, and outsourced-product costs must close before showing the formal grand total; aftersales remains explicitly cumulative. Finance owns material and workshop unit-rate maintenance and order cost recalculation; procurement records only the offline procurement deadline completion, and cost completion never blocks production.
- The order administrator performs both pre-review and technical approval; there is no dedicated technical-review employee or `TECH_REVIEWER` role. Technical approval never collects installation fees. During every manufactured shipment registration, the order administrator records exactly one installation fee for each involved area; multiple same-area routes in one vehicle share that fee, and first/later batches use the same rule. Installation staff may only add the shipment transport fee when completing installation. Preserve historical technical-package first-batch fee fields for audit, but they enter cost only after an order administrator confirms the value into a shipment area.
- Shipment commands never accept a client-selected delivery date. The server records the Asia/Shanghai current calendar date for manufactured, outsourced, and combined shipments while preserving a precise audit timestamp.
- Procurement may finalize all active areas' hardware-package totals in one atomic order transaction. Every area is present, zero is valid, final totals cannot be below current valid cumulative counts, and the server appends only each difference. Finalized sources stay read-only until explicitly reopened with a reason.
- A workshop supervisor may bulk-complete ordinary processes only inside one requested production area/route and their own workshop; chairman-administrators may explicitly select the workshop. Apply predecessor order, keep one report per process, skip hardware handoff/package-count ledgers, reject unresolved issues, and recalculate route/area/order/task projections only after all writes succeed.
- `BUSINESS_COMPLETE` remains the persisted terminal workflow state. The first aftersales case derives a visible thirteenth `售后` step and `售后处理中` marker without reopening the order. All active aftersales accounts share the completed-order queue. With the cost feature enabled, aftersales staff and chairman-administrators register each aftersales problem together with its initial immutable dated cost batch in one transaction; they may append later batches or void a batch with a reason. Finance and chairman see the four-part order cost, aftersales staff see only aftersales-cost details, and aftersales attachments are limited to those three roles.
- Order risk counts must be derived from visible risk facts. Order detail must show each risk's cause, impact, responsible role, and resolution route; never show an unexplained scalar risk count.
- Material unit costs and order material cost details are visible only to procurement, finance/cost, and the chairman-administrator; only procurement and the chairman-administrator may create price versions or confirm procurement completion.
- All order-detail status surfaces read from one workflow state. Completing an action updates the lifecycle, order list, current owner, next task, risk count, operation record and dependent gates together.
- Dashboard risk summaries must state exactly which persisted exception types they count; show deadline reminders and overdue tasks separately until they are backed by the same risk projection.
- `ROUTE_V2` manufactured shipment selects immutable, completed production routes whose final batch has not been confirmed after tail-payment confirmation. One vehicle may combine routes from the same or different areas and ready outsourced products. A route may have multiple immutable shipment lines; the order administrator explicitly marks whether each selected route is in its final batch, and a final-batch line closes later shipment for that route. The server groups route lines into area batch facts and closes an area only after every applicable route has a final-batch line. Every manufactured vehicle records its installation team, site contact, planned installation date and site conditions in the shipment transaction, then creates one shipment-scoped installation task; historical order-level installation applications remain read-only and are never a gate. `AREA_LEGACY` retains its area-batch final marker. Customer acceptance requires every applicable route or legacy area to be fully shipped, every manufactured shipment installed, and every applicable outsourced product shipped.
- Order package count equals area-scoped hardware packages entered once by procurement plus `area × route` manufactured warehouse packages and per-product outsourced warehouse packages entered cumulatively by assembly. Late packages may be added after an earlier shipment while fulfillment continues, and package-count completeness never blocks shipment, installation, acceptance, or business completion. Shipment registration directly references the current total. Historical application/shipment counts remain read-only under the legacy basis and are never backfilled as new-source facts.
- Shipment-stage tail-payment work must also appear in the responsible employee's personal task queue: before reporting it belongs only to the order's assigned salesperson, then after reporting it disappears from sales and appears for finance verification. Derive this live from the same order payment facts so the detail owner, action button and personal queue cannot disagree.
- Formal workshop process templates keep the names from `流程图(2).xls`. Panel, wood and paint remain cumulative area reporting. `贴皮` is optional per production area and defaults off: 开料→制作1, then 贴皮 and 制作2 may run in parallel; 送油漆 waits for 制作2 and, only when created, 贴皮. Every `ROUTE_V2` route owns its assembly steps: step 1 `打包`; step 3 `入库` opens after packing and records that route's manufactured packages; step 4 `订单生产完成` is a quantity-free route milestone. Step 2 `领五金` reflects the one shared area-scoped hardware package handoff on both routes, is excluded from progress/completion, and never gates shipment. Package ledgers are also excluded from progress and route completion. Do not add unlike units into one workshop quantity or percentage. Outsourced warehouse package recording remains separate.
